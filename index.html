About Will

The full picture

I'm an actor, lighting designer, and writer based in Los Angeles. My background is in technical theater — I trained at Carleton College, worked as an electrician alongside professional touring companies, directed my senior thesis, and spent years as a lighting designer and operator across mainstage and independent productions.

Since moving to LA I've co-founded Big Egg Productions, an independent film company producing short films for competition and festival circuits. I'm currently volunteering as an electrician on a community theater production and building toward a full-time career in entertainment and live events.

Outside of production: I write long-form fantasy fiction, build elaborate fictional worlds, play competitive Valorant, and follow the kind of genre storytelling that rewards readers who pay close attention. My favorite author is Brandon Sanderson — the Cosmere is the fictional universe I care most about. Narrative games. Work where the universe itself has been thought through.
